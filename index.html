Every player has his own passing, dribbling, finishing, defending, pace and strength. On each action the man on the ball chooses to pass, dribble, cross or shoot, and the result depends on him, the defender in front of him, the club's tactics (press, defensive line, tempo, directness, set pieces, counters), the expected goals of the chance, a small home advantage and how each side's day is going. Squads and coaches are as of 1 October 2026. Ratings, player traits and tactical profiles are my own estimates, and the tactics are my reading of each coach's style. Each player also starts with a fitness level, shown as the small bar beside his position. It drains with work rate, pressing and tempo, makes him slower and less sharp as it falls, raises his injury risk, and decides who the manager takes off. The same match number always replays the same way.
